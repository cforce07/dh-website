# 1. Executive Brief

DirectHired is a Singapore-based domestic helper employment agency.

The company specialises in:

- Indonesian helpers
- Myanmar helpers
- Mizoram helpers

The company may expand into additional helper sources in the future, but the V1 website should focus on the currently established markets.

DirectHired's core philosophy is:

> **Happy Employer. Happy Helper.**

The company was established because the founders saw families struggling with traditional agency experiences where agencies often focus on **filling vacancies** rather than understanding whether a particular helper is actually suitable for the family's needs.

DirectHired takes the opposite approach:

> **Understand first. Recommend second.**

The website should communicate that DirectHired does not simply try to fill an available position.

Instead, DirectHired takes time to understand:

- The family
- The household
- The employer's requirements
- The required skills
- The family's lifestyle
- The type of helper needed

before recommending suitable helpers.

The website must therefore communicate three primary brand pillars:

### 1. Better Matching

Understand the employer's needs before recommending a helper.

### 2. Transparent Pricing

Provide clear package pricing and reduce uncertainty around what the employer is paying for.

### 3. Personalised Service

Provide human guidance rather than treating customers as another vacancy/order.

- Clear
- Confident
- Warm
- Professional
- Human
- Transparent
- Concise

- Corporate jargon
- Excessive marketing language
- Overly emotional copy
- Exaggerated claims
- Fake urgency
- Cheap promotional language

Prefer:

> "We take the time to understand your needs."

over:

> "Our revolutionary proprietary matching methodology guarantees the perfect domestic helper."

Never promise a "perfect match."

# 82. Design Principle

The most important design principle is:

> **Trust before conversion.**

The user should first think:

> "These people understand families."

Then:

> "Their pricing is clear."

Then:

> "They look credible."

Then:

> **"I'll submit my requirements."**

Do not overwhelm the user with conversion buttons before establishing trust.

---

# 83. Secondary Design Principle

Another important principle:

> **Show, don't over-explain.**

Instead of writing long paragraphs about better matching:

Show:

**Family Needs**

↓

**DirectHired Understanding**

↓

**Suitable Helper**

Use visual storytelling wherever possible.

---

# 84. Third Design Principle

> **Premium does not mean complicated.**

The interface should be simple enough for a first-time employer to understand immediately.

A user should be able to answer within seconds:

1. What does DirectHired do?
2. Why are they different?
3. How much does it cost?
4. How do I get started?
