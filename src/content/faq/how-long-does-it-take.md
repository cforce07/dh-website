---
question: How long does the process take?
surfaces: [faq, home]
category: process
order: 80
---

Once you have confirmed your helper, it takes approximately **2 weeks** for a helper coming to Singapore from overseas, and approximately **1 week** for a transfer helper who is already here.

Those timings run from confirmation onward. How long it takes to reach that point depends on your requirements and on the interviews — we would rather find the right person than rush you to a decision.
