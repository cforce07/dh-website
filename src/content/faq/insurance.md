Yes. DirectHired arranges the helper’s insurance as part of your placement, so it is not cover you have to go and source yourself.

There are **two** required policies, and the package buys both:

- **Medical insurance** — in-patient care and day surgery, with an annual claim limit of at least $60,000.
- **Personal accident insurance** — a sum assured of at least $60,000.

Both figures are the **minimums the Ministry of Manpower requires** of every employer of a migrant domestic worker. They are not limits DirectHired has set, and they are a floor rather than a description: what our policies pay above that floor, and on what conditions, are terms of the policy rather than of the package, and this answer does not set them out.

The insurance sits inside the fly-in package rather than beside it — it is one of the package’s own line items, and you can see what it costs on our [Pricing](/pricing) page.
