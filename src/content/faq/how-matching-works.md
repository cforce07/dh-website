question: How does the matching process work?
surfaces: [home, faq]
order: 5
---

Matching is a personalised, human process. Your consultant will:

1. Understand your family and household
2. Understand the type of helper and skills you need
3. Recommend suitable candidates based on your requirements
4. Support you through interviews so you and the helper can decide together
5. Stay involved to support the placement afterward

We take the time to understand your needs and recommend accordingly.
