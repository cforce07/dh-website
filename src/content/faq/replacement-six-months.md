---
question: What does the 6-month replacement mean?
surfaces: [faq, pricing]
category: replacement
order: 7
---

Our fly-in package with replacement entitles you to **one replacement helper**, requested within **6 months of your helper’s deployment date**.

You do not need to justify the request. If the placement is not working for your household, you can ask for a replacement and we will start the process. The one exception is where the employer has breached the Employment Act or has abused the helper.

Two details worth being clear about: it is one replacement rather than an unlimited number, and the 6 months runs from the deployment date. The package without replacement does not include this.
